# Discourse sheaf

## Definition

A [[cellular-sheaf]] read semantically: vertex stalk = private opinion space of an agent; edge stalk = discourse space of a channel, i.e. the space of things that can be *said* there; restriction map = [[expression-map]] taking a belief to its utterance [S5].

Nothing changes mathematically and everything changes interpretively [S5]. Public agreement becomes a statement about images under expression maps, not about beliefs, so a discourse sheaf can model consensus-in-speech over persistent disagreement-in-belief [S5][S41]. This is the most directly transferable idea in the wiki for LLM agents, whose "opinions" and "messages" are also different objects [S41].
